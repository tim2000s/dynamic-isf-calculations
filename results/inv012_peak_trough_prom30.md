# Peak-to-trough ISF x TDD by midpoint glucose

Generated by `inv012/peak_trough.py`. Cohort median of per-person bin medians,
95% bootstrap interval over people.
"Meal by bolus" is the share of people with no carbohydrate records, for whom a bolus of 5% of the daily dose stood in for a meal.

The median over every event was declared primary before the first run. It is unstable near target, where the insulin used is zero or negative in a large share of events and the ratio changes sign, so the comparison with the published shape uses the median over events with positive insulin used. That change was made after seeing the first run. The fitted line was added at the same time.
