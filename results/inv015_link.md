# INV-015: profile ISF or daily dose as the scale of the equation

Generated by `inv015/link.py`. Isolated calculator corrections in REPLACE-BG, where no
algorithm doses. The achieved sensitivity is the six-hour fall per unit given, put on the
footing of 180 mg/dL by the published shape. It is an outcome ratio and its level is not
read. Intervals are 95% over people.

150 adults with 5 or more corrections, 3433 corrections. Their entered
sensitivity x daily dose has a median of 1929, with the tenth and ninetieth percentiles at
1345 and 2997. Entered sensitivity and daily dose correlate at -0.73 in logs.
