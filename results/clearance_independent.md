# Exploratory clearance decomposition using an action-balance proxy

79 users, 57,866 insulin-active windows. Proxy action = ΔIOB + suggested SMB + ∫(suggested temp−profile basal). IOB is model-derived and suggestions may differ from enacted delivery. Low-action closed-loop windows do not identify non-insulin clearance.

## Effective ISF ratio: net vs clearance-corrected (insulin-only)

| BG band | net | clearance-corrected |
|---|---|---|
| 100-120 | 0.2 | 0.11 |
| 120-145 | 0.37 | 0.13 |
| 145-175 | 0.47 | 0.13 |
| 175-205 | 0.53 | 0.09 |
| 205-260 | 0.45 | 0.09 |

Implied k: net **-0.37**, corrected **0.78** (k>0 = falls with glucose = resistance).

The exploratory curves are net k=-0.37 and adjusted k=0.78. The design does not identify clearance or physiological insulin resistance, regardless of their difference.
