governed by `../DYNAMIC-ISF-AUDIT-2026-09.md`.

comparisons within the loop prediction model. Delta-IOB, starting-IOB and action-balance estimates

or insulin-independent clearance.

recommendations. The post-COB result remains an association consistent with residual carbohydrate
