Where do the numbers come from?

The paper

The scripts behind each number are at github.com/tim2000s/dynamic-isf-calculations, with the paper, “An alternative proposal for dynamic ISF in open-source AID systems”.

The curve

Romero-Ugalde et al., Diabetes 2025;74(Supplement 1):920-P (doi:10.2337/db25-920-P).

The logarithmic model

Chris Wilson’s talk to Loop and Learn on 6 February 2022 (youtube.com/watch?v=oL49FhOts3c).

Simulator and margins

Kovatchev et al. (2009) and Xie’s Simglucose (2018) for the simulator, and Battelino et al. (2019) for the consensus limits.

Tim Street, Diabettech. No participant data is included in the repository.

Everything in the deck comes from the paper, and each number in the paper comes from a script committed in the repository. The repository holds the code and the results and no participant data. The published curve is cited from the ADA abstract. If anything here looks wrong, the scripts are the place to check it, and I’d be glad to hear about it.
