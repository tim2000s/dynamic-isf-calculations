How far can we trust the simulator?

Insulin does more as glucose rises

In TimSim a unit of insulin lowers glucose further the higher glucose is. The equation’s premise runs the other way.

Profiles sit lower than adults enter

The titrated profiles have a median ISF × TDD of 988. Adults enter about 1,915.

Neither has been shown to be the cause. The run ranks rules inside that simulator, so the harm reading is reported and looked into, and it doesn’t stop the move to shadow running.

The simulation’s sizes are not estimates for people.

It’s fair to ask how much weight the simulation should carry, and I’d say some, with care. There are two known departures. In TimSim the true effect of a unit of insulin gets larger as glucose rises between 100 and 300 mg/dL, where the published equation says it gets smaller. So a rule that doses harder at high glucose is being tested in a body that responds more strongly there. And the simulated people’s titrated profiles have a median ISF × TDD of 988, against the 1,915 that adults enter. I haven’t shown that either of these produced the harm reading. What the run does give is a ranking of rules inside that simulator, and its sizes aren’t estimates for people. The reading is reported and has been looked into, and the place for a stop is the shadow and device records in real use.
