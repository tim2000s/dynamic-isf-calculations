Limits of the simulator

Insulin does more as glucose rises

In TimSim a unit of insulin lowers glucose further the higher glucose is, which is the opposite direction to the published curve.

Profiles sit lower than adults enter

The titrated profiles have a median ISF × TDD of 988. Adults enter about 1,915.

Neither has been shown to be the cause. The run ranks rules inside that simulator. The harm reading is reported and has been looked into, and it does not stop the move to shadow running.

The simulation’s sizes are not estimates for people.

TimSim departs from the published picture in two known ways. The true effect of a unit of insulin in TimSim gets larger as glucose rises between 100 and 300 mg/dL, where the published equation has it getting smaller. A rule that doses harder at high glucose was therefore tested in a body that responds more strongly there. The simulated people’s titrated profiles also have a median ISF × TDD of 988, against the 1,915 that adults enter. Neither departure has been shown to produce the harm reading. The run gives a ranking of rules inside that simulator, and its sizes are not estimates for people. The reading is reported and has been looked into. A stop belongs to the shadow and device records in real use.
