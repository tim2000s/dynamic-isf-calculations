Origins of dynamic ISF

Logarithmic

Chris Wilson collected about 300 data points from around 50 volunteers, who reported their correction doses and the effects. The model has been in AndroidAPS since February 2022.

Sigmoid

Trio’s sigmoid came from its author’s observation of glucose response. No analysis of data has been published in support of it.

Neither model drew on a large measured dataset. Diabeloop published a measurement from 11,201 adults in 2025.

Chris Wilson’s talk to Loop and Learn, 6 February 2022, and the AndroidAPS source history.

Both existing models were built by people in the community with the data available at the time. Chris Wilson ran an online form that about 50 volunteers answered. It gave roughly 300 data points of doses and effects, reported from experience and unmeasured. A neural network fitted to them gave a rule close to 277,700 ÷ (glucose × TDD). That rule went into AndroidAPS in February 2022, and the logarithm followed in June 2022. Trio’s sigmoid came from its author’s observation of glucose response. Both models describe a real effect: a unit of insulin appears to lower glucose less at higher glucose. A published measurement of that effect on a much larger group now exists.
