What did Diabeloop publish?

Romero-Ugalde and colleagues measured the fall in glucose per unit of insulin in 11,201 adults on Diabeloop’s DBLG1. It was 79 mg/dL at a glucose of 100, 35 at 180 and under 20 above 300.

Between 100 and 200 mg/dL that’s a fall by a factor of 2.61. The logarithmic model in AndroidAPS falls by 1.48 over the same range.

Romero-Ugalde et al., Diabetes 2025;74(Supplement 1):920-P (doi:10.2337/db25-920-P).

This is the curve the proposal rests on. It was presented at the ADA Scientific Sessions in 2025 and it’s a quartic in glucose. The authors suggest halving the dose at 120 mg/dL or below and possibly doubling it above 340, both relative to 180. If we look at the range most corrections happen in, 100 to 200 mg/dL, the published curve falls by a factor of 2.61 and the logarithmic model with a divisor of 55 falls by 1.48, so the published one is a good deal steeper.
