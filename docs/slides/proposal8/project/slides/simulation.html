Results in simulation

In TimSim, 40 simulated adults ran oref0 for two months, and only the sensitivity rule differed between arms.

Against oref0 with autosens, time below 54 mg/dL rose by 0.37 points (0.24 to 0.51), which reads harm.

Time above 250 mg/dL fell by 0.31 points (0.05 to 0.59), which reads cleared.

Against a logarithmic rule matched at 140 mg/dL, time below 54 mg/dL rose by 0.17 points (0.04 to 0.32), which reads unresolved.

Percentage points of time. The margins are 0.25 and 1.25 points, a quarter of the consensus limits (Battelino et al. 2019).

In TimSim, 40 simulated adults ran the shipped oref0 under a harm rule written before the runs. Over two months pooled, against oref0 with autosens, time below 54 mg/dL rose by 0.37 percentage points (0.24 to 0.51). The interval lies above zero and the mean is above the 0.25 margin, so the low side reads harm. Time above 250 mg/dL fell by 0.31 points (0.05 to 0.59), which clears the high side. Against a logarithmic rule matched to the same value at 140 mg/dL, time below 54 mg/dL rose by 0.17 points (0.04 to 0.32), which is unresolved, and time above 250 mg/dL changed by +0.09 (−0.12 to 0.29), which is cleared. Against the unchanged profile over both months, time below 54 mg/dL rose by 0.35 points (0.20 to 0.50) and mean glucose was 2.12 mg/dL lower (1.22 to 3.02). In the first month the rise in time below 54 mg/dL was about the size produced by a profile ISF entered 30% too low, which was +0.39 (0.22 to 0.56).
