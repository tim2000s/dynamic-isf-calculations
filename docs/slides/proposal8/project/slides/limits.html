The ends of the curve sit outside Trio’s default limits. Trio keeps the ratio to the profile between 0.7 and 1.2 unless the setting is changed. The curve stays inside that band only between 109 and 158 mg/dL, which is 49 of the 280 mg/dL it covers. At 300 mg/dL it asks for 2.41 times the profile’s insulin, and at 380 for 3.33 times. For the worked person, a correction from 300 to 100 mg/dL is 9.62 U from the curve and 4.80 U inside the limits. Running the curve as published would need limits of about 0.63 and 3.33, or none for this mode, and that is for Trio’s maintainers to decide. In simulation, running inside the limits changed time below 54 mg/dL by +0.13 percentage points (−0.02 to 0.27) and time in range by +0.90 (0.11 to 1.69) against the unchanged profile.
