The ends of the curve sit outside Trio’s default limits, and that needs saying plainly. Trio keeps the ratio to the profile between 0.7 and 1.2 unless you change it. The curve stays inside that band only between 109 and 158 mg/dL, which is 49 of the 280 mg/dL it covers. At 300 it asks for 2.41 times the profile’s insulin and at 380 it asks for 3.33 times. For the worked person, a correction from 300 down to 100 is 9.62 units from the curve and 4.80 inside the limits. To run as published the limits would need to be about 0.63 and 3.33, or absent for this mode. That’s for Trio’s maintainers to decide. In simulation, running inside the limits gave +0.13 points below 54 mg/dL (−0.02 to 0.27) and +0.90 points of time in range (0.11 to 1.69) against the unchanged profile.
