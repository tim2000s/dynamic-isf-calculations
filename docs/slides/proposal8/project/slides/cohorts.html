Ratio of sensitivity at 100 mg/dL to sensitivity at 200 mg/dL, measured within each person.

The measurement was repeated to see whether the effect is particular to Diabeloop. Across 1,790 people in eight cohorts, the within-person ratio between 100 and 200 mg/dL was between 2.2 and 4.0, against the published 2.61. The REPLACE-BG adults dosed from a pump calculator with no algorithm, and their ratio was 2.55 (2.25 to 2.82), which agrees with the publication. Loop gave 3.26 (3.11 to 3.39), and no single scale fitted the Loop data. Above 200 mg/dL the data were flatter than the published fit by one measure and agreed with it by the other. The top of the curve is therefore less well supported than the middle.
