Ratio of sensitivity at 100 mg/dL to sensitivity at 200 mg/dL, measured within each person.

The obvious question is whether this is a Diabeloop effect or something we’d see elsewhere. Across 1,790 people in eight cohorts, the within-person ratio between 100 and 200 mg/dL came out between 2.2 and 4.0, against the published 2.61. REPLACE-BG is the cleanest of them because those adults dosed from a pump calculator with no algorithm, and it gave 2.55 (2.25 to 2.82), which agrees with the publication. Loop came out at 3.26 (3.11 to 3.39), and under Loop no single scale fits. Above 200 mg/dL the data are flatter than the published fit by one measure and agree with it by the other, so I’d treat the top of the curve as less well supported than the middle.
