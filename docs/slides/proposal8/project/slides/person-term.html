The missing term for the person

The published curve has no term for the person. A person on 20 U a day and a person on 80 U a day both get 50.9 mg/dL per unit at 140 mg/dL.

Scale by profile ISF

The curve passes through the person’s own profile ISF at a chosen glucose and keeps its published shape on either side.

Scale by TDD

The height comes from total daily dose, as it does in AndroidAPS and Trio now. Adult settings, with ISF × TDD near 1,915, would put those two people at about 96 and 24 mg/dL per unit.

Recorded corrections can show which of the two fits what people do.

ISF × TDD was 1,915 (1,828 to 1,986) in 422 adults.

The equation is a population curve. It returns 35.3 mg/dL per unit at 180 mg/dL for everyone, and 50.9 at 140 for a person on 20 U a day and for a person on 80. Adults’ entered settings put ISF × TDD at 1,915 (1,828 to 1,986) in 422 adults, which would give those two people about 96 and 24 mg/dL per unit. The curve therefore needs a scale for the person before it can be dosed from, and there are two candidates. One is the profile ISF the person already has. The other is total daily dose, which the existing dynamic ISF models in AndroidAPS and Trio use. The rest of the deck is about which of the two the curve is scaled by, and the curve itself is taken as given.
