A between-person comparison in 150 REPLACE-BG adults giving their own corrections. People using a loop and children were not tested.

REPLACE-BG holds adults giving their own corrections from a pump calculator, with no algorithm dosing. Scaling by profile ISF predicts a coefficient of 1 on the entered ISF and 0 on daily dose. Scaling by TDD predicts 0 and −1. The measured coefficients were 0.95 (0.55 to 1.32) on the entered ISF and −0.07 (−0.43 to 0.26) on daily dose. TDD is therefore excluded as the scale between people unless about three quarters of the six-hour fall was independent of the dose. It is a comparison between adults giving their own corrections, and it has not been tested in people using a loop or in children.
