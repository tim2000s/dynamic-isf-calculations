Profile ISF or TDD?

The test used 3,433 corrections from 150 adults in REPLACE-BG, each given by the person with no algorithm dosing.

0.95

Coefficient on the entered ISF (0.55 to 1.32), where the profile link predicts 1 and the TDD link predicts 0

−0.07

Coefficient on daily dose (−0.43 to 0.26), where the profile link predicts 0 and the TDD link predicts −1

A between-person comparison in 150 REPLACE-BG adults giving their own corrections. Loop users and children were not tested.

This is the test that decided it. REPLACE-BG has adults giving their own corrections from a pump calculator, so there’s no algorithm dosing in the way. If the fall in glucose follows the person’s entered ISF, the coefficient on ISF should be 1 and the one on daily dose should be 0. If it follows TDD, they should be 0 and −1. What came out was 0.95 (0.55 to 1.32) on the entered ISF and −0.07 (−0.43 to 0.26) on daily dose. The TDD link is excluded unless about three quarters of the six-hour fall was independent of the dose. Having said that, it has limits. It’s a comparison between people, in adults giving their own corrections, and it hasn’t been tested in people using a loop or in children.
