From “What is not settled” in the paper.

There’s a fair amount that isn’t settled and I’d sooner list it than have someone find it. The profile link was tested between adults giving their own corrections, so it hasn’t been shown within a person, in people using a loop, or in children. Whether a multiplier for recent dosing belongs on top is open. The anchor is 140 by my choice, and the publication’s 180 would make every dose 1.44 times smaller. The two ends of the curve are both open questions: whether the steep part between 100 and 150 mg/dL should be dosed from, and how far to follow the fall above 200, where the other cohorts were less clear. I haven’t asked the authors about four details of their method, or whether they’re content for the equation to be built into open-source code. And nothing has been tested in people.
