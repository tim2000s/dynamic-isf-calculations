What is not settled

The scale

It was tested only between adults giving their own corrections. People using a loop and children were not tested.

The anchor

140 mg/dL is a modelling choice. The publication’s reference is 180 mg/dL, which would make every dose 1.44 times smaller.

The ends of the curve

It is open whether the steep part between 100 and 150 mg/dL should be dosed from, and how far to follow the fall above 200 mg/dL.

The authors

They have not yet been asked about four details of the method, or whether the equation may be built into open-source code.

People

Nothing here has been tested in people. The evidence so far comes from recorded data and simulation.

Taken from the section “What is not settled” in the paper.

Scaling by profile ISF was tested between adults giving their own corrections, so it has not been shown within a person. People using a loop and children were not tested. Whether a multiplier for recent dosing belongs on top is open. The anchor at 140 mg/dL is a modelling choice, and the publication’s 180 would make every dose 1.44 times smaller. Both ends of the curve are open: whether the steep part between 100 and 150 mg/dL should be dosed from, and how far to follow the fall above 200 mg/dL, where the other cohorts were less clear. The authors have not yet been asked about four details of their method, or whether they are content for the equation to be built into open-source code. Nothing has been tested in people.
